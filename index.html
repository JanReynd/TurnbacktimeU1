TEACHER NOTES

One journey, four skills.

Designed for B1–B2 EFL learners. Suggested pacing: vocabulary 15 minutes, reading 15 minutes, research 30–40 minutes, then reporting in pairs or small groups.

Source: the teacher-supplied Turn Back Time workbook extract, printed pages 37–41 (sections 4.1–4.3). The reading is an original shortened adaptation of the included Karl Sergison account, BBC programme information and family recruitment advertisement. Exercise sentences, questions and the sports mission were created for this app.

The historical shop scenes describe the programme’s recreation, not universal rules for every historical shop. The family advertisement is a reading text, not a current invitation to apply.

Research: pupils use an AI tool approved by their teacher outside this app. Ask them to open source pages and check the exact claim, date and context. Sources should be independent where possible. A completed notebook does not automatically mean its claims are accurate.

Progress: stored only in this browser, with no class dashboard or online submission. Downloads and printouts can be handed in through your usual classroom system. There is no automatic assessment of speech or historical accuracy.

Classroom use: the standalone HTML works offline; internet access is needed for AI research and external sources. Upload this single index.html file to a static web host, or open it directly in a modern browser. Progress stays with the browser and address used; it does not sync between devices.
